Small hardware is the category most garages give up on organizing entirely. Screws, nuts, bolts, and washers are too small for bins, too varied for a single container, and too easy to just dump into a coffee can "for now" — a system that quietly turns into years of digging through mixed hardware every time a project needs one specific screw.

These three products solve it at different scales: a full drawer cabinet for a real hardware collection, a wall-mounted bin system for the middle ground, and small stackable bins for overflow or a second category entirely.

## How to choose small parts storage that actually works

Match the system to how often the hardware gets touched: daily-use hardware belongs somewhere visible like a wall-mounted bin system, while a larger but less-frequently-accessed collection is better served by labeled drawers where volume matters more than instant visibility. Buy slightly more capacity than the current collection needs — hardware storage is one of the few garage categories that reliably grows over time as projects accumulate leftover pieces. And resist the urge to sort by project; sorting by hardware type (screw size, bolt diameter) holds up much better over years than a system tied to specific past projects.

## FAQ

**What's the best way to organize screws, nuts, and bolts that are all mixed together?**
Start by sorting into rough categories — screws, bolts, nuts, washers — before assigning drawers or bins, since sorting mixed hardware directly into a final system usually means redoing it once you see how much of each type you actually have.

**Should small hardware storage be wall-mounted or in a drawer cabinet?**
Both, ideally — wall-mounted bins for hardware used constantly and worth having visible, drawer cabinets for a larger collection where hiding it behind labeled fronts keeps the workbench area from getting visually cluttered.

**How do I stop small parts storage from becoming disorganized again?**
Keep a couple of bins or drawers empty as designated overflow space — most small parts systems break down not from bad sorting but from running out of room and hardware getting dumped wherever there's space left.
