## How to choose under-sink storage that actually works

Measure before buying anything rigid — this is the one cabinet in the kitchen where plumbing genuinely dictates what fits, and a product that looks perfect online can be unusable if it doesn't clear your specific pipe layout. Split the cabinet into zones (front, back, door) and solve each separately rather than looking for one product to fix the whole space — the front, back, and door all fail in different ways and need different fixes. And favor pull-out and turntable mechanisms over fixed shelving wherever pipes allow it, since "reach in and grab" is the actual daily-use pattern this cabinet needs to support.

## FAQ

**What's the best under-sink organizer for a cabinet with a lot of plumbing?**
An L-shaped pull-out, specifically — it's designed to wrap around a center drainpipe rather than requiring a clear rectangular space, which most other organizer styles need.

**How do I stop things from getting lost in the back of an under-sink cabinet?**
A turntable is the direct fix — spinning the back-corner items forward is easier than any amount of front-zone reorganizing, since the core problem is depth and reach, not clutter.

**Can I use adhesive hooks inside a cabinet door if it's humid under my sink?**
Most damage-free adhesive hooks hold up fine to typical under-sink moisture, but avoid mounting them anywhere that gets directly wet from leaks or condensation — check the door surface is dry and clean before applying.
